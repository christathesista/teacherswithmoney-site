what it is

A cozy weekend party about teacher money.

A

side a · day 1, saturday

Getting your money right.

The basics nobody handed you with your first contract: how teacher pay actually works, what to do with it, and why money feels heavier than it should. Every session ends with one small step you can take that day.

B

side b · day 2, sunday

Growing income around the pay scale.

The pay scale is fixed. Your income isn't. Sunday is educators sharing how they built income around a teaching schedule, beginner level on purpose, with zero pressure to copy any of it. Ask the questions you'd never ask in the staff lounge. This room is kind.

We keep pushing for better teacher pay AND we kick ass in the meantime.

Each session is around 20 minutes, pre-recorded and released across the weekend, so you can watch when it suits you.

questions

Questions at the door.

Is it free?

Yes!! The live weekend is free, funded by sponsors so it stays that way. If you want the sessions for keeps, the all-access pass keeps every video open after the weekend, plus workbooks, session notes, a private podcast feed, and more fun things I'm still packing in. Pricing lands when registration opens on December 28.

Do I have to watch live?

No. Sessions release across the weekend and each one stays up for 48 hours. If you want them longer than that, the all-access pass is for you.

Is this financial advice?

No. The Summit is education, not individualized advice. Nobody here will tell you what to do inside your own accounts, and nothing here is therapy. For your own situation, talk with a qualified professional.

Is there a paid option?

Yes, and it's a good one!! The all-access pass (the Deluxe Edition) keeps every session open after the 48 hours are up, so you can rewatch the one that hit, catch the ones you missed, and come back in July when the thing you were avoiding is suddenly urgent. It comes with workbooks, session notes, a private podcast feed, and more fun things. Pass details and pricing drop when registration opens on December 28.
