The weekend, in a nutshell

The Teachers With Money Summit is a free, virtual weekend of teacher money basics, without the overwhelm. It's the very first one, and I'd love for you to be part of it.

Free to attend, with a paid upgrade. Every educator can register and watch for free. Attendees who want to keep the sessions can buy the optional All-Access Pass. More on that below, because the All-Access Pass is great news for you!

It's for educators who feel overwhelmed by their money and are ready to finally look at it: classroom teachers, specialists, admins and former teachers. Most of them were never taught how their own money works, and a lot of them have been avoiding it because of that. This weekend gives them a friendly, judgment-free place to start.

I'm Christa, host of the First in Class podcast and founder of Teachers With Money. Here's how the weekend works:

  • Saturday, January 30 is about getting your money right: the basics, explained in plain language.
  • Sunday, January 31 is about building income around the pay scale. The pay scale is fixed. Your income isn't.

  • It's free for every educator. Sponsors fund the weekend so it stays that way.
  • Sunday ends with a live Money Date, where attendees pick one money task and actually do it, together. Then I close out the weekend.
  • Attendees who want to keep the sessions after the weekend can buy the All-Access Pass. It includes ongoing access to every session, notes and worksheets, and bonuses from the speakers.

Content guidelines

These keep the weekend safe and welcoming for every attendee, and they keep all of us clear of giving financial advice.

  • Teach, don't advise

    Share how things work and what people can consider. Please don't tell attendees what to do with their own money, like which account to open or what to invest in.

  • Keep product pitches out of your {{ITEM}}

    Please don't recommend or pitch specific retirement-account or investment products. Mentioning your freebie is totally welcome.

  • No promised results

    Your story and real examples are great! Please don't promise attendees a specific income, savings number or result.

  • Every path is welcome

    Our attendees are on lots of different paths, whether they stay in the classroom, move into a new role or have already left. Please skip "quit teaching" framing, so nobody feels like the weekend isn't for them.
