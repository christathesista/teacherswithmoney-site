Our pillars

What we talk about around here

Five pillars guide everything we make at Teachers With Money, from the podcast to the newsletter to the Summit.

Side Hustles

The pay scale is fixed. Your income isn't. We talk about building income around your teaching schedule, on your terms, with real numbers from educators who've done it.

Wealth Strategy

Budgeting a ten-month paycheck across twelve months, saving, and understanding your benefits, explained in plain language. This is education, never personal financial advice.

Money and Mental Health

Money stress is real, and you don't have to carry it alone. We talk honestly about the guilt, shame, and overwhelm that can come with money, and we pair every conversation with one small, doable step. For the clinical side, we bring in licensed experts.

Teacher Pathways

Stay in the classroom, step into leadership, or take your skills somewhere new. Every path gets the same respect and the same possibility.

Systemic Change

Teachers deserve better pay. We push for that, AND we help you grow your income in the meantime.
