can't find it?

New senders sometimes land in the wrong spot. Check the Promotions tab if you use Gmail, and the spam or junk folder anywhere else.

When you find it, tell your inbox I'm a friend. In Gmail, drag the email from Promotions into Primary, or open it, tap the three dots, and choose "Move to Primary". In other inboxes, mark it "Not spam" and add the sender to your contacts.

Do that once and the real invites land where you'll see them.
