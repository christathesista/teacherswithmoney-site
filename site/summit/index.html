what it is

Two days. One for your money, one for your income.

day 1, saturday

Getting your money right.

The basics nobody handed you with your first contract: how teacher pay actually works, what to do with it, and why money feels heavier than it should. Every session ends with one small step you can take that day.

day 2, sunday

Growing income around the pay scale.

The pay scale is fixed. Your income isn't. Sunday is about building income around the schedule, beginner level on purpose, and then picking your one thing before the weekend ends.

Sessions are pre-recorded and released across the weekend, so you can watch when it suits you. Captions on every video.

who's invited

Class Acts of every kind.

Classroom teachers. Specialists. Admins. Paras. Former teachers. Edupreneurs. If you work in education, or you did, you're on the list.

If you were never taught how your own money works, and you've been avoiding it a little (or a lot), this weekend was built for you. No shame here, no lectures. Just a weekend of catching up on the stuff we should have been taught, together.

the lineup

The lineup is coming together right now.

Invitations are out. As each speaker says yes, they show up here.

why it's a party

Picture a party at my house.

I'm not running a conference. I'm filling a room with my favorite people in teacher money and introducing you to all of them. That's my whole job that weekend.

Come in your coziest sweatpants. Bring a teacher friend. Stay for the parts you need and skip the rest, because money stuff is way more fun with company.

questions

The short answers.

Is it free?

Yes. The live weekend is free, funded by sponsors so it stays that way.

Do I have to watch live?

No. Sessions are released across the weekend, and the replays are free for a limited window. [REPLAY WINDOW TBD]

Is this financial advice?

No. The Summit is education, not individualized advice. Nobody here will tell you what to do inside your own accounts, and nothing here is therapy. For your own situation, talk with a qualified professional.

Is there a paid option?

An all-access pass with ongoing access is coming.
