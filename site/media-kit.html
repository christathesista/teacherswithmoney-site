About

Christa taught elementary music for ten years and left the classroom in 2023. She built a top 1% Teachers Pay Teachers store, founded Teachers With Money, and hosts the weekly First in Class podcast. Since 2025 she has coached 20+ educators one on one. She speaks at conferences, universities, schools, and district wellness days.
